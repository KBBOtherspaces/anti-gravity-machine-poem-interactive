There are articles online
about letting go.

Deciding to Let Go
The Practice of Letting Go
Mastering the Art of Letting Go
How to Actually Let Go
Letting Go of Letting Go

I don’t know
if people actually wrote these,
or tried them, or if they work.

In the 4th Season Finale
of The Office, Michael said,
“I want to fly through the air
without this horrible weight
holding me back.”

I used to say I would
have a Goodbye Toby party
when I never had to see
my ex husband again.
Once the Arizona courts
had set it so, to move
forward I was told, “Now,
you have to forgive him.”

Tony Robbins has 13 ways
to let go. The men have
all the answers.
The bones of the past, for me,
have a specific face, no soul
behind its dead eyes
hollow and expired
I left the carcass
in the desert, not far
from where I found it.

I don't believe
in raising the dead
So I don't need forgiveness
I let that shit go.
